# Backride Dates: 100 Date Cards

**Working title.** Swap the name once you lock it.

A monthly couple ride series. One wheel, one spin, one Date Card. All ride times are one way from Camarin, North Caloocan, riding two-up at a moderate pace.

---

## How the Date Card works

Every wedge on the wheel is just a place name, colour-coded by direction. When it lands, the card reveals:

> **PLACE** (Direction)
> **Activity:** what you do there
> **Budget:** the cash in the envelope

Three lines. No rules to explain.

## Budget tiers

Budget is tied to distance and entrance costs so it feels fair, not random.

| Tier | Ride time | Budget | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Malapit | Under 2 hours | ₱1,500 | Gas, simple meal, low or no entrance |
| Katamtaman | 2 to 2.5 hours | ₱2,000 | Gas, toll, entrance, proper meal |
| Malayo | 2.5 to 3.5 hours | ₱2,500 | Gas, toll, activity fee, meal, snacks |
| Sagad | 3.5 hours and up | ₱3,000 | Everything above plus boat, resort, or guide fees |

Everything comes out of the envelope. Count what's left on camera at the end.

## Direction colours

| Direction | Provinces | Personality |
|---|---|---|
| **North** | Bulacan, Nueva Ecija, Tarlac, Pampanga, Pangasinan | Easiest and cheapest, expressway most of the way |
| **West** | Bataan, Zambales, Subic | Coast and sunsets, best-looking footage |
| **East** | Rizal, Quezon, Marilaque | Closest, most road-focused, good for tight months |
| **South** | Cavite, Batangas, Laguna, south Quezon | Costs the most time, you cross the metro first |

# Practical notes

**Book ahead:** Masungi Georeserve, Las Casas Filipinas, Mt. Pinatubo 4x4, Hundred Islands boats. Masungi in particular needs weeks of lead time, so slot it deliberately rather than leaving it to the wheel.

**Seasonal:** Pawikan Conservation Center only has turtle releases roughly November to February. Tayug sunflowers bloom on a schedule. Tinipak, Daraitan and the Zambales river crossings are miserable or closed in heavy rain.

**Rainy season, June to October:** weight the wheel toward North and East, and toward the shorter rides. Save the coastal West and the long South trips for the dry months.

**Two-up comfort:** cap regular episodes at 3 hours one way, stop every 45 to 60 minutes, and sort out a backrest and proper pillion gear before episode one. Everything on this list is achievable, but a tired passenger ends a series faster than bad footage.

**Repeat shot:** take the exact same photo pose at every destination. Same framing, same pose. By episode 12 the grid is the most shareable thing you will make all year.
